## Context

From January to August 2024 I interned as a Software Development Engineer at Zeta, working on
UPI payment services. This is employer work, so it is described here in prose only.

## What I did

- **Removed a legacy dependency.** I refactored more than 250 files to remove the legacy Gateway
  Commons dependency, which made the services more modular and the builds more stable.
- **Cut transaction latency by 20%.** I introduced caching (AsyncCache) in the VPA validation
  flows.
- **Fixed security vulnerabilities.** I fixed more than 10 critical vulnerabilities found with
  Snyk, keeping the services compliant with RBI regulations.
- **Regulatory limits.** I implemented MCC restrictions and ₹2 lakh transaction caps.
- **Fewer failed QR payments.** I relaxed over-strict validation logic, which improved the QR
  scan success rate and reduced transaction failures.
