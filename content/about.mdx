I'm a backend software engineer at Zeta in Bangalore, where I build the systems behind card
payment disputes, chargebacks and merchant refunds. It is work where correctness matters more
than cleverness: a duplicated claim or a mismatched refund is a real customer's money.

Most of what I enjoy is turning fragile, hard-coded flows into designs that are safe to retry and
easy to extend: making dispute creation idempotent, replacing live service lookups with batch
reconciliation, and moving card-network rules into configuration so a new network is an addition,
not a rewrite.

I studied Computer Engineering at Delhi Technological University (2020–2024) and have solved more
than 800 DSA problems along the way.
